# Customer understanding research — ready to run, not performed

Recruit at least 15 UK adults across age, financial confidence and digital confidence. Include people who use screen readers and people who request extra support. Provide compensation independent of purchase or answers. Do not recruit using pressure or promises of cover.

Participant consent script: “We are testing a fictional insurance website, not you. No insurance is sold. You can stop or skip any question. Please use fictional personal details and do not disclose diagnoses, passwords or card details. With your separate permission we will record the session for research. [Research controller/contact/retention TBC].” Obtain explicit consent before recording; supply accessible information and withdrawal routes.

| Task | Open question, without prompting | Passing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Read a displayed price | What would leave your account each month? Where is tax shown? | Identifies total and that IPT is already included |
| Compare excess options | If an eligible bill were £1,000 with £250 excess, what would you need to check? | Understands own share and application period/limits |
| Choose underwriting | Explain the two methods in your own words. Do either guarantee existing-condition cover? | Understands timing and no guarantee |
| Read exclusions | Would this replace A&E or ongoing chronic-condition care? | Recognises NHS/emergency route and exclusions |
| Review cancellation | When can you cancel and where would you check refund rules? | Finds approved wording and 14-day context |
| Ask for help | How would you pause or ask for another format? | Finds support and understands demo service limits |

Score each: 0 incorrect, 1 partial, 2 clear and unaided. Record completion time, misunderstood wording, confidence and access barriers. Do not interpret a high total as permission to ignore a critical misunderstanding. Proposed gate: no unresolved critical misunderstanding of tax, exclusions or excess; compliance/research owner must approve threshold before collecting data.

Before/after sheet fields: pseudonymous participant code; accessibility accommodation; design version; task; score; verbatim observation with consent and redaction; issue; change; second version; retest score; owner/date. Compare equivalent cohorts and tasks. Do not fabricate participant results. All 15+ real sessions and retesting remain outstanding.
