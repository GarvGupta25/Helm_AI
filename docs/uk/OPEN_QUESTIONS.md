# Owner decisions and launch blockers

The engineering work uses a demo-only posture until these are resolved.

1. Legal entity, registered office, company number, FRN or principal firm, ICO number and evidence of status.
2. Panel scope, remuneration, commission and fees.
3. Real launch versus portfolio demo; currently persistent demo labelling is mandatory.
4. Payment arrangements and authorised payment service; no real money is collected.
5. Welsh and other language requirements; English only until professionally reviewed translations exist.
6. Approved licensed photography and captioned explainer video; do not fabricate provenance.
7. Real statistics, testimonials and founder quote; omit unverified claims.
8. Sex-for-rating legal assessment; omitted from the new journey.
9. Business cover distribution and advice permissions.
10. Hosting, inference region, processor agreements and international transfer mechanism.
11. Named compliance, legal and data protection owners.
12. Approval for migrating historical policy data and archived professional workspaces. Historical financial units cannot safely be relabelled as another currency.
13. Recruit at least 15 UK participants for comprehension testing, plus NVDA/VoiceOver and real-device testers.

No software test substitutes for legal sign-off, real-user research, or an actual database backup and migration rehearsal.
