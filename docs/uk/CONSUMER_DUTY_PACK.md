# Consumer Duty evidence pack — not a conformance claim

Owner: compliance [TBC]. No real customer outcomes are claimed.

Monitor: understanding of tax-inclusive premium, annual excess, underwriting and exclusions; drop-off by form step; accessibility errors; complaint themes; repeat confusion; support availability. Collect only necessary, consented event metadata; never diagnoses, free text or precise financial vulnerability details.

Financial-promotion checklist for each route: identify audience and purpose; place exclusions beside benefit statements; substantiate statistics; show total tax-inclusive price; avoid implied FCA status; label fictional plans; disclose panel/ranking/remuneration; no urgency or fake reviews; provide usable support; test comprehension; record reviewer/date/version and remediation. Existing pages are draft until reviewed by a qualified owner.

Vulnerability policy draft: permit pauses and back navigation; simple language; no pressure; suppress sales on distress/bereavement; do not ask for health details in chat; offer accessible formats and trusted-person involvement with permission. A demo ticket is not human support. Live escalation needs staffing, SLA, role-based access, consented transcripts and monitoring.

Fair-value work: obtain genuine product costs, benefits, target-market and remuneration evidence from carriers; assess product limitations for each target group; monitor outcomes. Synthetic price tables cannot establish fair value.

Required approvals: compliance, legal, DPO, product owner. Attach evidence references, dates and decision rationale; unresolved [TBC] means no approval.
